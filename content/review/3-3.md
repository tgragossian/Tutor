## `openstax-c1-3.3-107` (exercise 107, difficulty 1)

- **Statement:** Find $f'(x)$ for $f(x) = 5x^3 - x + 1$.
- **Answer:** $15x^2-1$
- **Wrong answers tested:** $15x^2$ (dropped the derivative of -x); $15x^2-x$ (differentiated only the cubic term)
- **File:** `content/problems/openstax-c1-3.3-107.yaml`

## `openstax-c1-3.3-109` (exercise 109, difficulty 1)

- **Statement:** Find $f'(x)$ for $f(x) = 8x^4 + 9x^2 - 1$.
- **Answer:** $32x^3+18x$
- **Wrong answers tested:** $32x^3+18x-1$ (constant term not dropped); $32x^4+18x^2$ (exponent not lowered)
- **File:** `content/problems/openstax-c1-3.3-109.yaml`

## `openstax-c1-3.3-113` (exercise 113, difficulty 2)

- **Statement:** Find $f'(x)$ for $f(x) = x^2\left(\frac{2}{x^2} + \frac{5}{x^3}\right)$.
- **Answer:** $-\frac{5}{x^2}$
- **Domain:** all reals except 0
- **Wrong answers tested:** $\frac{5}{x^2}$ (sign error: derivative of 1/x taken as +1/x^2); $2x\left(-\frac{4}{x^3}-\frac{15}{x^4}\right)$ (product of the derivatives)
- **File:** `content/problems/openstax-c1-3.3-113.yaml`

## `openstax-c1-3.3-137` (exercise 137, difficulty 3)

- **Statement:** Find an equation of the tangent line to the graph of $f(x) = 2x^3 + 4x^2 - 5x - 3$ at $x = -1$. Enter the right-hand side of $y = \dots$.
- **Answer:** $-7x-3$
- **Wrong answers tested:** $-7x+4$ (used f(-1) = 4 as the y-intercept); $-7x$ (left out the point entirely)
- **File:** `content/problems/openstax-c1-3.3-137.yaml`

## `openstax-c1-3.3-145` (exercise 145, difficulty 3)

- **Statement:** A herring swimming along a straight line has traveled $s(t) = \frac{t^2}{t^2 + 2}$ feet in $t$ seconds. Determine the velocity of the herring, in ft/s, when it has traveled 3 seconds. Give an exact answer or round to 4 decimal places.
- **Answer:** $\frac{12}{121}$ (round to 4 places)
- **Wrong answers tested:** $\frac{9}{11}$ (reported the position s(3) instead of the velocity); $\frac{6}{11}$ (quotient rule numerator terms subtracted the wrong way)
- **File:** `content/problems/openstax-c1-3.3-145.yaml`
