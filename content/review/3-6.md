## `adapted-c1-3.6-sinx2` (author-written (adapted), difficulty 1)

- **Statement:** Which is the derivative of $y = \sin(x^2)$?
  - (a) $2x\cos(x^2)$ ← correct
  - (b) $\cos(x^2)$
  - (c) $\cos(2x)$
  - (d) $2x\sin(x^2)$
- **Wrong answers tested:** $b$ (missing inner derivative 2x); $c$ (differentiated the inside in place); $d$ (derivative of sin taken as sin)

## `openstax-c1-3.6-223` (exercise 223, difficulty 3)

- **Statement:** Find $\frac{dy}{dx}$ as a function of $x$ for $y = \left(\frac{x}{7} + \frac{7}{x}\right)^7$.
- **Answer:** $7\left(\frac{x}{7}+\frac{7}{x}\right)^6\left(\frac{1}{7}-\frac{7}{x^2}\right)$
- **Domain:** all reals except 0
- **Wrong answers tested:** $7\left(\frac{x}{7}+\frac{7}{x}\right)^6$ (missing inner derivative); $7\left(\frac{x}{7}+\frac{7}{x}\right)^6\left(\frac{1}{7}+\frac{7}{x^2}\right)$ (sign error: derivative of 7/x taken as +7/x^2)
- **File:** `content/problems/openstax-c1-3.6-223.yaml`

## `openstax-c1-3.6-229` (exercise 229, difficulty 2)

- **Statement:** Find $\frac{dy}{dx}$ for $y = (5 - 2x)^{-2}$.
- **Answer:** $\frac{4}{(5-2x)^3}$
- **Domain:** all reals except 5/2
- **Wrong answers tested:** $-\frac{2}{(5-2x)^3}$ (missing inner derivative -2); $-\frac{4}{(5-2x)^3}$ (sign error in the inner derivative)
- **File:** `content/problems/openstax-c1-3.6-229.yaml`

## `openstax-c1-3.6-231` (exercise 231, difficulty 2)

- **Statement:** Find $\frac{dy}{dx}$ for $y = (2x^3 - x^2 + 6x + 1)^3$.
- **Answer:** $6(2x^3-x^2+6x+1)^2(3x^2-x+3)$
- **Wrong answers tested:** $3(2x^3-x^2+6x+1)^2$ (missing inner derivative); $3(6x^2-2x+6)^2$ (differentiated inside the power instead of using the chain rule)
- **File:** `content/problems/openstax-c1-3.6-231.yaml`
