## `openstax-c1-3.5-185` (exercise 185, difficulty 2)

- **Statement:** Find an equation of the tangent line to $f(x) = -\sin x$ at $x = 0$. Enter the right-hand side of $y = \dots$.
- **Answer:** $-x$
- **Wrong answers tested:** $x$ (sign error: derivative of -sin x taken as +cos x); $-1$ (slope only, no line)
- **File:** `content/problems/openstax-c1-3.5-185.yaml`

## `openstax-c1-3.5-187` (exercise 187, difficulty 3)

- **Statement:** Find an equation of the tangent line to $f(x) = 1 + \cos x$ at $x = \frac{3\pi}{2}$. Enter the right-hand side of $y = \dots$.
- **Answer:** $x+1-\frac{3\pi}{2}$
- **Wrong answers tested:** $x-\frac{3\pi}{2}$ (left out f(3pi/2) = 1); $-x+1+\frac{3\pi}{2}$ (sign error: derivative of cos x taken as +sin x)
- **File:** `content/problems/openstax-c1-3.5-187.yaml`

## `openstax-c1-3.5-191` (exercise 191, difficulty 3)

- **Statement:** Find $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}$ for $y = x\sin x - \cos x$.
- **Answer:** $3\cos(x)-x\sin(x)$
- **Wrong answers tested:** $2\sin(x)+x\cos(x)$ (stopped at the first derivative); $\cos(x)-x\sin(x)$ (product rule applied only once in the second derivative)
- **File:** `content/problems/openstax-c1-3.5-191.yaml`

## `openstax-c1-3.5-193` (exercise 193, difficulty 2)

- **Statement:** Find $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}$ for $y = x - \frac{1}{2}\sin x$.
- **Answer:** $\frac{1}{2}\sin(x)$
- **Wrong answers tested:** $-\frac{1}{2}\sin(x)$ (sign error in the second derivative of sin x); $1-\frac{1}{2}\cos(x)$ (stopped at the first derivative)
- **File:** `content/problems/openstax-c1-3.5-193.yaml`

## `openstax-c1-3.5-209` (exercise 209, difficulty 2)

- **Statement:** Find $\frac{d^3y}{dx^3}$ for $y = 3\cos x$.
- **Answer:** $3\sin(x)$
- **Wrong answers tested:** $-3\sin(x)$ (stopped at the first derivative); $-3\cos(x)$ (stopped at the second derivative)
- **File:** `content/problems/openstax-c1-3.5-209.yaml`
